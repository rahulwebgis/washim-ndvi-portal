How the portal works

The portal reads cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs (COGs) directly in your browser, so every number you see is computed from the actual Sentinel-2 or Landsat pixels over Washim. Nothing is pre-processed on a server.

Workflow

  1. Area of interest. Pick the whole district, one of the six talukas (Washim, Malegaon, Risod, Mangrulpir, Manora, Karanja), any of the 792 villages, or draw or upload your own polygon.
  2. Imagery. Search Sentinel-2 L2A (10 m, every 5 days) or Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 (30 m, every 8 days combined). Tiles acquired on the same date are mosaicked automatically. You can also point at any STAC API, paste a STAC item or COG URL, or upload your own GeoTIFF.
  3. Compute. Choose an index and pixel size. Clouds, shadows and cirrus are masked with the Sentinel-2 Scene Classification layer or the Landsat QA_PIXEL bits. Pick a second scene B to map change.
  4. Read the results. The results panel lists area statistics, the histogram, area per class and mean values for every village and taluka. Click the map for pixel values.
  5. Time series. Build a monthly series over any area and read the Vegetation Condition Index drought classes and monthly anomalies.
  6. Report. The composer places map, legend, north arrow, scale bar, locator map, charts and tables on an A4/A3 page. Drag and resize each element, then export PDF, PNG or JPG, or print.

Drought classes

NDVI classes on the map are indicative thresholds for Deccan-plateau rainfed cropland (soybean, cotton, tur). The time-series tab uses the Vegetation Condition Index, VCI = (NDVI − NDVImin) / (NDVImax − NDVImin) × 100 (Kogan, 1995): below 10 extreme, 10–20 severe, 20–30 moderate, 30–40 mild, 40 and above no drought. Compare the same crop season across years (for example Kharif, September) for the most reliable reading.

Land surface temperature

LST uses the Landsat Collection 2 surface-temperature product (ST_B10: × 0.00341802 + 149.0 K, converted to °C). For an uploaded thermal band in brightness temperature, tick the emissivity option and the portal applies an NDVI-threshold emissivity correction.

Hosting

The portal is a static site. Put the folder on GitHub Pages, Netlify or any web server. It needs internet access to reach the STAC catalogues and imagery; uploads work offline.
